Full List of Improvements

Moved to the new directory/file structure introduced in LetsChat upstream with version 6.1, when 6.1 released NovaChat decided to not immediately switch to the new structure but instead maintain the old one and backport patches from the new structure into the old one. Now that NovaChat has finally moved to this new structure, it no longer needs to spend extra effort in backporting changes but can instantly pull upstream changes into the Nightly branch with no extra work.

Phase 4 of the redesign is out, it is the final phase of the redesign and is more of an improvement/enhhancement to what already came with the previous phases.

All legacy pages from LetsChat Social have recieved their final updates, they won't be recieving any new changes after this and since their dependencies are on the files from the old directory structure they have been linked to the new Kwitter-compatible files so that they can recieve security updates from the rest of NovaChat. Though they aren't guaranteed any security updates when it comes to security holes that specifically affect them since they are now EOL. They won't be recieving the Phase 4 redesign and will be stuck on phase 3 indefinetely, all files from the old directory structure now instead of depending on each other depend on the 6.1 compatible files and all the discontinued pages now lead to the pages in modern day NovaChat as they have the updated Navigation menu with the updated links.
